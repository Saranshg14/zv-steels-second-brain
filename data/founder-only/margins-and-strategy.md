# Margins and Walk-Away Points

Minimum acceptable gross margin: 12% on any order. Below this, we walk away.

For orders above 50 tonnes, we can negotiate down to 10% if the client is strategic (repeat potential, referral value, or anchor account for a new region).

Never go below 10% under any circumstances. If a client is pushing us there, they are not our client.

Current cost benchmarks (internal — do not share with clients):
- TMT bars: cost + 14% is our standard sell price.
- Structural sections: cost + 16%.
- Plates and sheets: cost + 13%.

These benchmarks are reviewed quarterly. Sales team does not have access to raw cost data.

# Supplier Strategy

Primary suppliers are on 60-day credit terms. Do not pay early unless there is a meaningful discount offered (minimum 1.5%).

We keep a minimum 3-week buffer stock of our top 5 SKUs. If buffer drops below 2 weeks, reorder immediately.

Never single-source any SKU. Always have a backup supplier qualified and ready. Switching cost should be less than 5 days lead time.

# Growth Targets (Confidential)

FY2027 target: 40% revenue growth over FY2026.
- Achieve by adding 3 new anchor accounts (above Rs. 50L annual spend each).
- Expand into 2 new cities — Pune and Coimbatore are priority.
- Margin target: maintain blended gross margin above 13.5%.

Share these numbers only in founder-level planning sessions. Do not share with sales team.
