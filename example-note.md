# Pricing for repeat customers
We give repeat customers 3% off on orders above a set size. Reason: repeat orders cost less to serve and we want long-term accounts.

# Late payments
If a payment is more than 15 days late, we pause new orders until the account is cleared. Reason: protect cash flow. Exceptions only with founder approval.
