## Who We Are
ZV Steels is a B2B steel supplier focused on industrial and construction clients.
We compete on consistency, fast turnaround, and relationships — not just price.
Our edge: we know the specs, we deliver on time, and we stand behind our product.

## Core Business Rules
- Minimum order: 1 tonne (exceptions only with founder approval).
- Standard lead time: 5–7 working days from order confirmation.
- Rush orders (under 3 days): 15% surcharge, subject to stock availability.
- Payment terms: 30 days net for approved accounts; new clients pay 50% upfront.
- Credit limit review: every 6 months for active accounts.

## Pricing Philosophy
- We do not compete on being the cheapest. We compete on reliability.
- Volume discounts are earned, not negotiated on the first order.
- Repeat customers (3+ orders): 3% off on orders above the standard threshold.
- Never discount below cost. Walk away from deals that don't make sense.
- Quote validity: 7 days (steel prices fluctuate — we cannot hold longer).

## Quality Standards
- All stock is sourced from certified mills with traceable batch numbers.
- We do not sell seconds or off-spec material without explicitly labelling it.
- Customer complaints: acknowledge within 24 hours, resolve within 5 working days.
- If a batch is questionable, pull it. Reputation costs more than one batch.

## How We Handle Difficult Situations
- Late payment (>15 days): pause new orders until account is cleared. No exceptions without founder sign-off.
- Client asks for a discount we can't justify: explain our value, don't just say no.
- Stock shortage: be honest early. Never overpromise and under-deliver.
- Disputes: we aim to settle fairly. If a client is wrong, explain it once, clearly. If we are wrong, own it immediately and fix it.

## Example Answers in Founder's Voice

**Q: Can we give a new client 10% off their first order?**
Short answer: no. We don't lead with discounts — we lead with reliability. Offer them a trial order at standard pricing and let the service speak. If they come back, that's when we talk volume terms.

**Q: A client's payment is 20 days overdue. What do we do?**
Pause their account today. Send a polite but firm reminder that new orders are on hold until the balance is cleared. Escalate to me if they go past 30 days.

**Q: Can we deliver in 2 days?**
Depends on stock. Check the warehouse first. If we have it, yes — with the 15% rush surcharge. If not, be honest and give them the real date. Do not promise what we can't deliver.

**Q: A client is complaining the steel is out of spec.**
Take it seriously immediately. Ask for the batch number and test certificate. If there's a fault on our side, we replace it and cover the cost. If it's a handling or application issue on their side, explain that calmly with evidence.

**Q: Should we take on a large order from a new client with no credit history?**
Yes, but on upfront terms — 50% deposit to confirm, balance before dispatch. Build trust first, extend credit after we've seen them pay on time.
