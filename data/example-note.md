# Pricing and Discounts

Standard pricing applies to all clients. We do not negotiate on the first order.

Volume discount structure:
- Orders 1–5 tonnes: standard rate
- Orders 6–20 tonnes: 2% off
- Orders above 20 tonnes: negotiate case by case (founder approval required)

Repeat customer discount: clients with 3 or more completed orders get 3% off on orders that meet the minimum threshold. This is automatic — sales team does not need to ask for approval.

Quote validity is 7 days. After that, we re-quote. Steel prices move — we cannot hold a price indefinitely.

# Payment Terms and Late Payments

Standard payment terms for approved accounts: 30 days net from invoice date.

New clients and unverified accounts: 50% upfront before order is confirmed, balance paid before dispatch.

Late payment policy:
- 1–14 days overdue: send a reminder, continue processing existing orders.
- 15+ days overdue: pause all new orders for that account immediately.
- 30+ days overdue: escalate to founder. Consider referring to collections.

Exceptions to the pause policy require founder sign-off only. Sales team cannot override this on their own.

Credit limits are reviewed every 6 months for active accounts. Clients who consistently pay early can request a limit increase.

# Lead Times and Rush Orders

Standard lead time: 5–7 working days from order confirmation and payment clearance.

Rush orders (delivery in under 3 working days):
- Subject to stock availability — check warehouse before committing.
- 15% surcharge applies automatically. This is non-negotiable.
- Sales must confirm with operations before promising a rush delivery to the client.

If we cannot meet a deadline honestly, say so early. Never overpromise. A late delivery that was promised costs us more in trust than losing the order.

Out-of-stock situations: inform the client immediately with a realistic date. Offer partial fulfilment if available. Do not go silent.

# Quality Standards and Complaints

All steel stock is sourced from certified mills. Every batch has a traceable batch number and mill test certificate (MTC). We do not sell off-spec or second-grade material without explicit labelling and client agreement.

If a client raises a quality complaint:
1. Acknowledge within 24 hours — even if we don't have an answer yet.
2. Ask for the batch number, MTC, and photos or test results if available.
3. Investigate within 2 working days.
4. If the fault is ours: replace the material and cover transport costs. No argument.
5. If the fault is the client's (handling, incorrect application): explain clearly with evidence, calmly.
6. Resolve all complaints within 5 working days.

If a batch in our warehouse is questionable, pull it immediately. Do not ship it while investigating. The cost of one bad batch is nothing compared to a reputation hit.
