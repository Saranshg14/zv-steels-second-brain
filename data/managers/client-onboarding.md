# New Client Onboarding and Credit

New clients go through a basic credit check before we extend 30-day terms.

Steps:
1. Client submits company registration and two trade references.
2. We verify references within 3 working days.
3. Starting credit limit: Rs. 5 lakhs for the first 6 months.
4. After 6 months of on-time payments: review and increase if justified.

Until credit is approved, all orders are on upfront terms (50% to confirm, balance before dispatch). Do not make exceptions to this — it protects our cash flow.

Large first orders (above 10 tonnes) from new clients: always escalate to founder before confirming, regardless of upfront payment.

# Account Management Guidelines

Active accounts should be reviewed quarterly by the sales manager:
- Payment history: are they paying on time?
- Order frequency: are they growing or shrinking?
- Credit utilisation: are they near their limit?

Flag any account that has gone more than 60 days without an order — check in before they go to a competitor.

Do not share credit limit information with the client directly. If asked, say it is reviewed periodically and they can request a review.
